# Process configuration

What each `process.*` setting in `config.yml` does, its unit, and what you should
expect to see when you change it. blah2 reads these once at startup, so a change
takes effect when blah2 restarts.

On a RETINA node, `config.yml` is generated by retina-node's config merger from
`default.yml`, the node's `user.yml` and `forced.yml`. Change values there, not in
the generated file.

Figures below assume the shipped capture: `fs` 2 MS/s, `cpi` 0.5 s, Doppler span
±1000 Hz, a 503 MHz illuminator (wavelength 0.596 m).

## Units used throughout

| Quantity | Value | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delay bin | 149.9 m of bistatic range | c / `fs` |
| Range resolution | about 195 m | c / 1.536 MHz, the RSPduo IF filter at 2 MS/s |
| Doppler bin | 2.0 Hz | 1 / `cpi` |
| Doppler to speed | Hz × wavelength = bistatic range rate (m/s) | 2.0 Hz is 1.19 m/s at 503 MHz |

Bistatic range is (transmitter to target) + (target to receiver) - (transmitter to
receiver). A target at distance R from the receiver has a bistatic range between
0 and 2R, depending on its direction.

## `process.detection`

The detector is a 1D cell-averaging CFAR. For each cell under test it averages
the power in `nTrain` cells either side along delay, skipping `nGuard` cells next
to it, and declares a detection when the cell exceeds that average by a factor
set by `pfa`. Each Doppler row is thresholded against its own training cells.

blah2 checks these values at startup. An invalid value stops blah2 before it
opens the SDR, with a log line such as `Error: process.detection: nTrain must be
1 or more`.

| Key | Default | Valid | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `enable` | true | true, false | |
| `pfa` | 0.00001 | greater than 0, less than 1 | probability per cell |
| `nGuard` | 2 | 0 or more | delay bins, each side |
| `nTrain` | 6 | 1 or more | delay bins, each side |
| `minDelay` | 5 | at most `maxDelay` | delay bins |
| `maxDelay` | no limit | at least `minDelay` | delay bins |
| `minDoppler` | 15 | 0 or more | Hz, absolute |
| `maxDoppler` | no limit | at least `minDoppler` | Hz, absolute |
| `nCentroid` | 6 | 0 to 65535 | bins, each side |

`maxDelay` and `maxDoppler` are optional. Leaving them out tests every cell,
which is how blah2 behaved before they existed.

### `pfa`

The chance that a noise-only cell is declared a detection. Lower means a higher
threshold: fewer false alarms, weaker targets missed.

Expected false alarms per CPI are roughly cells tested × `pfa`. At the shipped
settings that is 986 Doppler rows × 396 delay columns × 1e-5, about 3.9 per CPI
before centroiding. With `nTrain` 6 (12 training cells) the threshold over the
local average is:

| `pfa` | Threshold |
| --- | --- |
| 1e-5 | 12.86 dB |
| 4e-5 | 12.02 dB |
| 1e-4 | 11.42 dB |

**Expect:** each 10x increase in `pfa` gives about 10x more false alarms, spread
evenly over the map, for about 1.4 dB more sensitivity.

### `nTrain` and `nGuard`

More training cells give a steadier noise estimate and a lower threshold for the
same `pfa`: 24 cells instead of 12 gives 11.70 dB instead of 12.86 dB. The cost
is that the estimate covers a wider stretch of delay, so it follows changes in
the noise floor less closely.

At the nearest delays the left-hand training cells sit on the direct-path skirt,
which on one measured node raised the floor by 6.3 dB at delay 0 and 1.5 dB at
delay 5. More training cells there raise the threshold for close targets.

`nGuard` stops a target's own spread from leaking into its training cells and
raising its threshold.

**Expect:** raising `nTrain` gives slightly more detections far out and slightly
fewer at the nearest delays.

### `minDoppler` and `maxDoppler`

Doppler rows with an absolute Doppler below `minDoppler` or above `maxDoppler`
are not tested. The limits are inclusive. The map itself is unchanged, and
training cells are not affected.

`minDoppler` keeps clutter near zero Doppler out of the detections. It also hides
every slow target. At 503 MHz, 15 Hz is a bistatic range rate of 8.9 m/s, so a
target slower than about 4.5 m/s can never be detected in any direction. 5 Hz lowers
that to 1.8 m/s.

The direct signal and still clutter sit at exactly 0 Hz, and the clutter filter
cancels them. What the guard really protects against is clutter that is not
quite still, such as wind-blown trees and slow vehicles, which spreads over a
few Hz. How much of that exists depends on the site.

**Expect:** lowering `minDoppler` adds detections in the newly tested rows first.
If those rows fill with detections while the rest of the map does not change,
the site has moving clutter at those Doppler values, and that is the floor for
this site.

`maxDoppler` limits detections to slower targets without narrowing the
processing. Use it in preference to narrowing `ambiguity.dopplerMin/Max`, which
folds fast targets into the remaining span as ghosts.

### `minDelay` and `maxDelay`

Delay columns below `minDelay` or above `maxDelay` are not tested. The limits are
inclusive.

`minDelay` 5 excludes everything under 750 m of bistatic range, where the
direct-path skirt raises the noise floor. It also hides targets close to the
receiver: at 375 m from the receiver, a target is inside 750 m of bistatic range
in almost every direction.

**Expect:** lowering `minDelay` adds detections at the nearest delays, where the
raised floor makes false alarms more likely than elsewhere.

### `nCentroid`

Merges duplicate detections of one target. A detection is removed when a
stronger one lies within `nCentroid` delay bins and `nCentroid` Doppler bins of
it. At 6, that is ±900 m and ±12 Hz.

**Expect:** lowering it keeps two close targets apart, but strong targets may
produce several detections. Raising it merges more, and can remove a weak target
that sits near a strong one.

## `process.ambiguity`

| Key | Default | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `delayMin` | -10 | delay bins |
| `delayMax` | 400 | delay bins |
| `dopplerMin` | -1000 | Hz |
| `dopplerMax` | 1000 | Hz |
| `dopplerWindow` | `none` | `none` or `hann` |

`delayMin` and `delayMax` set the delay extent of the map, which is also the
extent the detector can test. The map is built by the batches algorithm, which
loses (nCorr - delay) / nCorr of a target's power, where nCorr is 999 at ±1000 Hz.
Keep `delayMax` well under nCorr. Lowering `delayMax` reduces the cells tested,
and with them the false alarms, but anything further away is not processed at
all. Keep `delayMin` at -10: the training cells at the nearest delays use the
negative bins.

`dopplerMin` and `dopplerMax` set the Doppler span. The batch rate equals the
span, so a target outside it folds back inside as a ghost 7 to 15 dB below its
true strength. At ±150 Hz, an aircraft at 250 Hz appears at -52 Hz, 14 dB down.
Inside the span, a target near the edge loses up to 3.9 dB. A wide span costs
slow targets almost nothing: 0.03 dB for a target at delay 7 and 30 Hz. On a
RETINA node the span is fleet policy and a `user.yml` override is dropped.

`dopplerWindow` tapers the batches before the Doppler FFT. Without it, a strong
target that falls between two Doppler bins leaks into its neighbours. With
`hann`, that leakage falls sharply: in the unit test, leakage 8 or more bins away
dropped from -23.4 dB to -40.3 dB. The costs are a 1.76 dB loss for every target,
and a wider peak, so a target spreads into the Doppler bins either side. The
window does not affect anything at exactly 0 Hz, which falls on a bin centre and
does not leak.

**Expect with `hann`:** fewer detections in the Doppler rows next to strong
targets and next to slow-moving clutter, and slightly fewer detections of the
weakest targets.

## `process.clutter`

| Key | Default | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `enable` | true | |
| `delayMin` | -10 | delay bins |
| `delayMax` | 400 | delay bins |

The clutter filter is an adaptive filter fitted once per CPI. It removes
everything in the surveillance channel that matches the reference signal at a
fixed delay between `delayMin` and `delayMax`: the direct signal and still
clutter. Anything that moves during the CPI survives. Anything still is removed,
including a hovering drone, which is cancelled like a building.

Turning `enable` off shows the uncancelled direct signal and clutter ridge along
0 Hz. That is useful as a diagnostic, but the detector will fire heavily near
0 Hz while it is off.

Reducing `delayMax` leaves clutter beyond it uncancelled. Increasing it makes the
filter's solve cost climb steeply.

## `process.data`

| Key | Default | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cpi` | 0.5 | seconds |
| `buffer` | 1.5 | CPIs of capture buffer |
| `overlap` | 0 | unused |

`cpi` sets the Doppler bin (1 / `cpi`) and the integration gain. Keep it at 0.5:
the clutter filter's FFT length is tuned for 0.5 s at 2 MS/s and falls back to a
slower length at any other value. 0.5 s is also about as long as a manoeuvring
small drone stays in one Doppler bin at 503 MHz.

`overlap` is not read by blah2.
